# Surface brief: Biyahe home page (island overlay + route board)

Scope: every HTML layer of the home page: island overlay (header plate, skip, contact, hint, stop strip, in-world stop signs and Open prompt, cursor, tap marker), the project card dialog, loader, and the Projects section below. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience, job and constraints: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The HTML is painted jeepney signage, not a website laid over a game. It refuses the frosted panel, SaaS pill and card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Yellow route plates with ink rims and a press-down ledge, jeepney-red stop strips, ink asphalt with yellow dashes, sky-peach ground, Fredoka for every label, Rubik for reading text, drawn SVG icons.
STORY: Visitors drive or tap to labelled landmarks, open real projects, and end at the Pier with Upwork and LinkedIn in reach.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Island full-bleed. Top-left a one-line route plate "Biyahe | Ian Joseph Quilatan, frontend developer". Top-right a red "Hire me on Upwork" plate and a quiet "Project list" plate. Each landmark wears a floating name plate; the parked stop's plate becomes "Open". Bottom: a compact route strip of stop plates on a dashed road.
FORM: User-pinned route board (chosen in the critique round); no concept-seed roll. Seed key: none.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
A tap drops pulsing chevrons on the grass; the jeepney sweeps round the landmark and its sign flips to "Open".
